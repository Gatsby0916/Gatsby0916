# Hi, I'm Haiyi Li 👋

Incoming **M.S. in Computational Science and Engineering** student at **Harvard University** (Fall 2027), currently on a gap year traveling the world.
Previously Mathematical Sciences (Honours) at the **University of Adelaide** and Mathematics & Applied Mathematics at the **Ocean University of China**.

I work where **applied mathematics meets computer graphics and human-AI interaction**: applied analysis and PDEs, numerical methods, 3D Gaussian Splatting, and data-driven human-AI systems. My goal is to build mathematically principled, stable, and interpretable models for real-world uncertainty.
